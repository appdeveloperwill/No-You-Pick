No You Pick - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

No You Pick ("the App") is a restaurant picker application that helps groups decide where to eat. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

What Data We Collect

Location Data (on your device only):

  • If you enable GPS: Your device's geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude) are obtained via your device's native location services.
  • If you enter a location manually: Your ZIP code, city, and state are stored locally on your device.

Preference Data (on your device only):

  • Cuisine type, group size, search radius, and number of restaurant choices — all stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences.

Important: We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data. Your location and preferences never leave your device.

How We Use Your Data

Your location coordinates are sent only to OpenStreetMap's Overpass API and Nominatim geocoding service directly from your device to retrieve nearby restaurant information. These queries are made peer-to-peer and do NOT pass through our servers.

Your preferences (cuisine, radius, etc.) are used locally on your device to filter and display restaurant results.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which are free and open-source:

  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API: Provides restaurant data from OpenStreetMap. See their privacy policy at https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap): Converts manual location addresses to coordinates. See their usage policy at https://nominatim.org/

Data Retention

All user preferences and settings are stored locally on your device. You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the App
  • Clearing the App's data via your device's Settings → Apps → No You Pick → Clear Data

No User Accounts, Tracking, or Ads

The App does NOT:

  • Require user registration or login
  • Track or analyze your usage
  • Display advertisements
  • Collect analytics or crash reports
  • Share your data with third parties (except Overpass/Nominatim for restaurant queries, as described above)

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. No registration or authentication is required.

Security

All communication between your device and third-party services (Overpass API, Nominatim) uses secure HTTPS connections.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the App, you can contact us through the Google Play Store listing or reach out directly.
